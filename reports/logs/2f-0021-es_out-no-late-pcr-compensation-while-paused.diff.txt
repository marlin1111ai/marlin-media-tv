Subject: [PATCH] input: es_out: do not compensate a late PCR while paused

While es_out is paused, EsOutDecodersStopBuffering anchors the input clock
of a rebuffer at the pause date, so that resume shifts it by the pause
length. A PCR read right after a seek taken while paused is then late by
about the time since the pause minus the pts delay. The late branch resets
the PCR: it flushes the decoders and rebuffers without moving the demuxer,
the new rebuffer is anchored at the pause date again, and the input laps
forward through the stream, raising the jitter up to clock-jitter, until
Play resumes from wherever the last lap left off instead of the seek target.

Nothing is output while paused, so there is no lateness to compensate. Skip
the compensation while paused, as is already done while frame stepping.
The pause-date anchor is unchanged.
